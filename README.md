# Desk Pal privacy policy

Last updated: 12 November 2026

Desk Pal is a desk wellbeing companion for Windows, made by MindtheApp. It reminds you to stretch, drink water, rest your eyes and take short walks. This page explains, in plain words, what the app does with your information. The short version: it has no accounts, no advertising, and does not collect usage data, analytics or telemetry. The only information that can leave your PC for us is a crash report, and only if you allow it.

## Crash reports

If Desk Pal crashes, it can prepare a crash report: the type of error and its message, the list of program functions that were running (the stack trace), the last lines of the app's log, the Desk Pal version, the Windows version and language, and basic device facts such as the processor type and the amount of memory. Before a report is stored or sent, file and folder paths are reduced to placeholders (for example `%USERPROFILE%`), and your user name, your PC's name and your IP address are removed. No files, screenshots or memory dumps are included.

You choose what happens in Settings, under "Send crash reports":

- **Ask** (the default): reports stay on your PC, in Desk Pal's data folder, and nothing is sent until you turn sending on.
- **Always**: reports are sent as they happen.
- **Never**: crash reporting is switched off completely and any report still waiting on your PC is deleted.

Reports are processed for us by Sentry (Functional Software, Inc.) and stored in its EU data region (Germany). They are kept for the retention period of our Sentry plan and then deleted automatically. Reports are not linked to your identity, are not used for advertising or tracking, and are not sold or shared with anyone else. To ask about or delete a report, contact us.

## What stays on your PC

Your settings, reminder schedule, daily progress, streaks, rewards and your companions' growth are stored only on your PC, in `%APPDATA%\DeskPal`. None of this is sent to us. To check whether you are in a call, or have Focus Assist or a full-screen app on, Desk Pal reads Windows' own status locally so it can hold reminders until a better moment. It does not record, listen to or look at anything, and this information never leaves your PC.

## Working with other apps

If you turn it on in Settings, Desk Pal can tell Automation Portal, another MindtheApp app on your PC, about events such as "break started" or "focus on", and Automation Portal can ask Desk Pal to start a break or pause. This happens only on your PC, through a local connection that other users of the PC cannot open. Those messages contain only reminder types, times and counts, never window titles, app names or calendar details. Both options are off until you turn them on, and you approve each app that wants to control Desk Pal.

## Children

Desk Pal is not aimed at children and does not knowingly collect personal information from anyone.
